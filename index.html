A helpful buying guide

Details that matter.

Jewellery kharidte waqt clear details dekhna zaroori hai. Final information hamesha apni purchase receipt par confirm karein.

Purity & Hallmark

Gold purity, hallmark aur HUID details (jahan lagu hon) check karein.

Weight Details

Gross weight, net metal weight aur stones ka weight alag se samjhein.

Price Break-up

Metal rate, making charges, wastage (agar ho) aur applicable taxes check karein.

Receipt & Exchange

Final amount, billing details aur exchange/buyback ki shartein pehle confirm karein.

Rates, charges and exchange terms can vary by design and purchase. Please check the final written bill before payment.
